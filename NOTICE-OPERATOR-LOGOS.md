# Operator logos

The files in `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/op_*.webp` are the logos of Iranian
mobile operators and ISPs. They are **trademarks of their respective owners** and are
shown in MarbleDU only to identify the operator a connection belongs to. MarbleDU is
not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these companies. If you are a rights holder
and want a logo removed or replaced, please open an issue.

## Sources

| Logos | Source |
|---|---|
| MCI, Irancell, Rightel, Shatel (also used for Shatel Mobile), Asiatech, HiWEB, Pars Online, MobinNet, SabaNet, Pishgaman, Zi-Tel, TCI | [`persianlabs/icons`](https://github.com/persianlabs/icons) (repository licensed MIT; logos remain trademarks of their owners) – rasterised from SVG, some cropped to the emblem |
| Taliya, ApTel, SamanTel, LotusTel, TeleKish, MTCE, Datak, Fanava, Afranet, Mabna Telecom, MahanNet, Laser, TIC, Respina | Publicly published logo images found via web image search (operator sites, Wikipedia, logo directories), trimmed and resized |

Anarestan and Petiak have no bundled logo yet (no trustworthy source was found); the
app falls back to the coloured letter badge for them and for any unknown ISP.
